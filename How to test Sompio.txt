HOW TO TEST SOMPIO
==================

Thank you for testing! Sompio is in open beta for Windows, Mac and Linux. Everything is
unlocked, free, until launch. Everything you put in it stays on your computer: nothing is sent
to us, and there's no account.

Download it from https://sompio.org/download.php. The page shows the steps for your system.


1. INSTALLING
-------------
Windows: run the setup file. Windows shows a blue "Windows protected your PC" box, because the
  beta isn't signed with a Microsoft certificate yet. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
Mac: open the .dmg and drag Sompio to Applications. The first time you open it, macOS blocks
  it: click Done, then System Settings, Privacy & Security, "Open Anyway".
Linux: make the AppImage runnable (right-click, Properties, "Allow executing file as program")
  and open it, or install the .deb with: sudo apt install ./Sompio_*_linux_amd64.deb

When Sompio starts, it walks you through the Sompio Lock in four steps:
  1. How the lock works: a short list of do's and don'ts. Please read it.
  2. Make your key picture: the button saves a .png file with a secret hidden in it.
     Put it somewhere you'll find it again (a copy off this computer is best).
  3. Choose your password.
  4. Write down or print the recovery code.

Please use made-up data while testing, not your real passwords or messages. The Samples page
has a made-up person, Alex Rivers, for everything (see step 2).


2. THE SAMPLES PAGE
-------------------
Open "Samples" in the menu on the left. "Add Alex's sample data" fills all three tools at once.
It also has, per tool:
  - Vault: sample entries (2FA codes, weak and reused passwords, auto-type tests), plus KeePass
    files and CSV exports to import. The KeePass sample password is: sample-password
  - Trace: open Trace with sample data, or save sample exports from Discord, Reddit, Telegram,
    X, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat, LinkedIn, WhatsApp and Gmail, and a sample
    computer folder with a screenshot and a photo in it.
  - Calendar: sample reminders (the first pop-up comes within a minute), a team schedule kept
    in New York time, and sample files.
Sample files are saved to a "Sompio samples" folder in your Documents, which opens for you.


3. WHAT TO TRY
--------------
Lock
  - Lock Sompio (Security page, or wait for the idle timer) and unlock it again.
  - Try a wrong password, and unlocking with the recovery code.
  - If you have a YubiKey: Security, "Add a YubiKey", then unlock with it.

Vault
  - Bring in passwords from your browser: Vault walks you through exporting them from Chrome,
    Edge, Brave or Firefox. (With sample data, import the sample CSV files instead.)
  - Folders on the left, entries in a table you can sort, the selected entry underneath.
  - Shortcuts: Ctrl+B copies the username, Ctrl+C the password, Ctrl+T the 2FA code, Ctrl+U
    opens the website, Ctrl+F searches. (On a Mac, Cmd instead of Ctrl.)
  - Health check: weak, reused, old and breached passwords, and passwords Trace found in your
    own chats (add Alex's sample data to see one).
  - The browser extension: in Vault, click "Set it up" and follow the steps. Then open a login
    page, click the Sompio button in the login field, and pick an entry. Lock Sompio and try
    again: the extension should say it's locked.
  - Windows only for now: auto-type. Open Notepad and press Ctrl+Alt+A.

Trace
  - Start screen: scan a folder (try the sample computer folder). Tick "Check where photos
    were taken", and on Windows "Read text in pictures": the sample screenshot and photo should
    show up as findings. Nothing is scanned unless you start it.
  - "I've asked" on a platform under "Ask for the rest": Sompio reminds you when the export
    should be ready.
  - Findings: pick a type on the left, click a row, try "Show values" and "Group repeats".
    Check that bank accounts, ID numbers and addresses from other countries are found.
  - "Clean up" goes through everything one at a time.
  - Requests: how to get your data from each platform, and letters that make companies
    delete it (please don't actually send one while testing).

Calendar
  - Type a reminder in the line at the top, like "Dentist Tue 15:00" or "Gym every Monday
    7:00", and press Enter.
  - Add sample reminders and wait for the pop-ups. Do they show up on time, without taking
    over what you're typing?
  - Close the window: Sompio keeps running (in the tray, or the menu bar on a Mac), and
    reminders keep coming, even when it's locked.
  - More: routines, schedules and groups, and "Import your phone's calendar" with a .ics file.


4. REPORTING A PROBLEM
----------------------
Use the feedback form: https://sompio.org/feedback.php

When something goes wrong, Sompio writes a short line about it to a problems log on your
computer. It only holds error messages, never your passwords, messages or files.
  1. Open Security (in the menu) and scroll to "Something wrong?".
  2. Click "Copy details for a bug report" and paste it into the feedback form.
  3. Add what you did and what happened. A screenshot helps a lot.

If Sompio won't start at all, the log is here:
  Windows: %LOCALAPPDATA%\app.sompio.desktop\problems.log
  Mac:     ~/Library/Application Support/app.sompio.desktop/problems.log
  Linux:   ~/.local/share/app.sompio.desktop/problems.log


5. NEW VERSIONS AND REMOVING SOMPIO
-----------------------------------
New versions come to you inside Sompio: a pop-up offers them, and your data stays as it is.

To remove Sompio:
  Windows: Settings, Apps, Sompio, Uninstall.
  Mac: drag Sompio from Applications to the Bin.
  Linux: delete the AppImage, or run: sudo apt remove sompio
Your encrypted data stays in the folder above (without problems.log) until you delete it.
