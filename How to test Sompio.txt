HOW TO TEST SOMPIO
==================

Thank you for testing! This is a test version of Sompio 0.9.0 for Windows 10 and 11.
Everything you put in it stays on your PC. Nothing is sent to us, and there's no account.


1. INSTALLING
-------------
Run Sompio_0.9.0_x64-setup.exe.

Windows will probably show a blue "Windows protected your PC" box. That's because this test
version isn't signed with a Microsoft certificate yet (the real release will be). To go on:
click "More info", then "Run anyway".

When Sompio starts, it walks you through the Sompio Lock in four steps:
  1. How the lock works: a short list of do's and don'ts. Please read it.
  2. Make your key picture: the button saves a .png file with a secret hidden in it.
     Put it somewhere you'll find it again (a copy off this PC is best).
  3. Choose your password.
  4. Write down or print the recovery code.
You get a 14-day trial of everything. No licence key is needed for testing.

Please use made-up data while testing, not your real passwords or messages. The Samples page
in the menu has made-up data for everything (see step 2).


2. THE SAMPLES PAGE
-------------------
Open "Samples" in the menu on the left. It has:
  - Calendar: sample reminders (the first pop-up comes within a minute), plus sample files.
  - Trace: open Trace with ready-made sample data, or save sample exports from Discord,
    Reddit, Telegram, X, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat, LinkedIn, WhatsApp and Gmail.
  - Vault: sample entries (2FA codes, weak and reused passwords, auto-type tests), plus KeePass
    files and CSV exports to import. The KeePass sample password is: sample-password
Sample files are saved to Documents\Sompio samples, and that folder opens for you.


3. WHAT TO TRY
--------------
Lock
  - Lock Sompio (Security page, or wait for the idle timer) and unlock it again.
  - Try a wrong password, and unlocking with the recovery code.
  - If you have a YubiKey: Security, "Add a YubiKey", then unlock with it.

Calendar
  - Add sample reminders and wait for the pop-ups. Do they show up on time, without taking
    over what you're typing?
  - Close the window: Sompio keeps running in the tray, and reminders keep coming, even when
    it's locked.
  - Make your own reminders, edit them, import the sample schedule files.

Trace (this used to be called Clean)
  - Open it with sample data. Findings shows everything found in one list: bank accounts,
    card numbers, passwords, addresses, phone numbers and more. Pick a type on the left,
    click a row to see the message underneath, and try "Show values" and "Group repeats".
  - "Clean up" in Findings goes through everything one at a time: it opens each message
    where it was posted, you delete it there and click "I deleted it, next".
  - The new Requests tab:
      Get your data: where and how to ask each platform for your data, with "I asked
        for it" (Calendar reminds you when it should be ready).
      Find it now: ready-made searches for your watch terms on Discord, Reddit and X.
      Ask a company: search a company (try Discord), pick "Delete my data", and look at
        the letter Trace writes. "Open in my email app" opens it ready to send; please
        don't actually send it while testing. Then "I've sent it: track the deadline".
      Your requests: the deadline, a reminder letter, and a complaint to the data
        protection authority.
  - Also go through Summary, Messages, Sources and Settings.
  - Import the sample exports under Sources, and try "Scan a folder on this PC" on the
    "Sample PC folder".
  - Add a watch term (in the sample, try "Sam Rivers"), mark things, delete things.

Vault
  - Add sample entries. It works like KeePassXC: folders on the left, entries in a table you
    can sort by clicking the column names, the selected entry underneath.
  - Try the KeePassXC shortcuts: Ctrl+B copies the username, Ctrl+C the password, Ctrl+T the
    2FA code, Ctrl+U opens the website, Ctrl+Shift+V types it, Ctrl+K clones, Ctrl+E edits,
    Ctrl+F searches, Delete deletes. Drag an entry onto a folder to move it.
  - The clipboard clears itself after a copy.
  - Website icons: when you save an entry with a website, Sompio gets the site's icon. If it
    can't, the entry shows a key. Click "Icon" on an entry to get it from another address
    (like blizzard.com for Battle.net), or choose a picture file. "Get icons for all entries"
    is under "Auto-type, import and export".
  - Health check, password generator, earlier versions of an entry, attachments.
  - NEW: the browser extension (Chrome, Edge, Brave or Firefox). Easiest: in Vault, click
    "Set it up" in the green bar and follow the three steps. By hand:
      1. In Sompio: Vault, the "Auto-type, import and export" tab, turn on "Let the Sompio
         extension use my vault". Copy the folder it shows.
      2. Chrome/Edge/Brave: open chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions), turn on
         Developer mode, click "Load unpacked" and choose that folder.
         Firefox: about:debugging, This Firefox, Load Temporary Add-on, pick manifest.json
         in that folder.
      3. Restart the browser. Click the Sompio button in the toolbar (pin it from the
         puzzle-piece menu), "Connect to Sompio", and click Allow in Sompio if the codes
         match.
      4. Add a sample entry with a real website's address (or use a sample), open that
         site's login page, click the Sompio button in the login field, and pick it.
      5. Sign in somewhere new (a made-up account on a test site is fine): Sompio should
         offer to save it. On a sign-up page, try "Use a strong password".
      6. Lock Sompio and try again: the extension should say it's locked.
  - Auto-type: open Notepad and press Ctrl+Alt+A. Sompio picks the "Notepad" sample entry
    and types it in. The test login page in the sample files works the same way. (The shortcut
    can be changed in Vault, "Auto-type, import and export".)
  - Import the sample KeePass files and CSV exports. Export, and open the export in KeePassXC
    if you have it.


4. REPORTING A PROBLEM
----------------------
When something goes wrong, Sompio writes a short line about it to a problems log on your PC.
It only holds error messages, never your passwords, messages or files.

To report a problem:
  1. Open Security (in the menu) and scroll to "Something wrong?".
  2. Click "Copy details for a bug report". That copies the Sompio version, your Windows
     version and the last lines of the problems log.
  3. Paste it in your message, and add what you did and what happened.
     A screenshot helps a lot (Windows key + Shift + S).

If Sompio won't start at all, the log is here (paste this into the File Explorer address bar):
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\app.sompio.desktop\problems.log


5. NEWER TEST VERSIONS AND REMOVING SOMPIO
------------------------------------------
To update, run the new setup file over the old one. Your data stays.

To remove Sompio: Windows Settings, Apps, Sompio, Uninstall. Your encrypted data stays in
%LOCALAPPDATA%\app.sompio.desktop unless you tick the box to delete it. Delete that folder to
remove everything.
